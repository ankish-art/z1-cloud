01Discover

Find out what actually happens, not what the procedure says.

Workflow DiscoveryEstablish what actually happens in your operations, as distinct from what the procedure says happens. 054–6 weeks

Overview

Discovery combines process mapping, observation, system log analysis and control walkthroughs.

Engagement

A fixed-duration discovery of four to six weeks, closed with a validation workshop.

Why it helps

  • An evidence-based view of current state rather than assumption
  • Early identification of control gaps and workaround risk

What you get

  • Validated current-state process maps
  • A register of risks and workarounds
  • A prioritised opportunity list with effort and control impact
Workflow TeardownA focused, time-boxed examination of one broken workflow, with a clear view of what to remove, simplify or protect. 062–3 weeks

Overview

A teardown examines a single process in depth: every step, handoff, system touch and approval, and whether each one earns its place.

Engagement

A two to three week teardown of one process with a small working group.

Why it helps

  • A clear view of which steps can be removed without control impact
  • Recognition of defensive steps that should be redesigned rather than repeated

What you get

  • A step-by-step inventory with keep, simplify or remove decisions
  • Quantified effort and cycle time impact
  • Redesign options with control implications stated

02Design

A target that technology, risk and operations can all sign.

Automation ArchitectureA target architecture for automation that technology, risk and operations teams can all sign. 07Review + reference design

Overview

We define how automation tools, data stores, AI services and integration layers fit together.

Engagement

An architecture review, a reference design and a governance pack covering change, access and resilience.

Why it helps

  • A shared architecture that reduces duplicated tooling
  • Explicit change control for workflow logic

What you get

  • A reference architecture and recorded design decisions
  • An access and change control model
  • A platform roadmap aligned to risk appetite
Operational TransformationRedesign how operations work end to end, so that controls, technology and people change together rather than in sequence. 03Phased programme

Overview

Operational change in regulated firms often fails because the process, the control framework and the system configuration are redesigned by different groups at different times.

Engagement

A phased programme with a discovery baseline, target operating model design and transition waves.

Why it helps

  • Fewer handoffs and rework loops across operational teams
  • Target operating models that second line can assess

What you get

  • A documented target operating model with control mapping
  • Fewer process variants and clear ownership for each step
  • Transition risk managed through staged cutovers

03Build

Automate the rules-driven steps without removing the controls.

Regulatory Workflow AutomationAutomate the high-volume, rules-driven steps of regulated work without removing the controls that make them defensible. 01Design sprint + staged build

Overview

Most regulated workflows are a sequence of checks, approvals and evidence requests that people perform by hand, across several tools, under time pressure.

Engagement

A fixed-scope design sprint followed by a build phase with control owners involved throughout.

Why it helps

  • Fewer manual touchpoints in high-volume processes such as screening reviews and periodic reviews
  • Consistent application of documented rules across teams and locations

What you get

  • Measurable reduction in cycle time for in-scope processes
  • Automated steps documented to a standard second line can test
  • A ranked backlog of automation candidates by control value and effort
System IntegrationConnect the systems that hold regulated data so information moves once, under controls, with an audit trail at each hop. 04Assess, design, build

Overview

Integration in regulated environments is less about connectors and more about data lineage, access control and failure handling.

Engagement

Integration assessment, a reference architecture, then build and test in environments that mirror production controls.

Why it helps

  • Reduced re-keying and the errors it introduces
  • Visible data lineage that supports regulatory queries

What you get

  • An integration map with owners and data classifications
  • Reconciliation controls with exception queues
  • Fewer manual data transfers between operational systems

04Govern

The model proposes. A named person or rule disposes.

Controlled AI WorkflowsIntroduce AI into operational processes with defined scope, review points and evidence at every decision. 02Pilot with go / no-go

Overview

We design AI-enabled workflows in which the model proposes and a named person or rule disposes.

Engagement

A use-case assessment, a controlled pilot with a defined exit criterion, then production design with the governance framework applied.

Why it helps

  • Faster first drafts of case narratives, summaries and document extraction, with reviewer sign-off
  • Clear boundaries on what AI may and may not decide

What you get

  • AI use cases approved for production with documented risk tiering
  • Reviewer effort reduced on routine items, with exceptions routed to people
  • A reusable pattern library for future AI workflows
AI Governance DesignDesign the controls, approvals and evidence that let your organisation use AI in regulated work with confidence. 084–6 weeks + rollout

Overview

AI governance is not a policy document placed on a shelf.

Engagement

Framework design over four to six weeks, followed by operational rollout support.

Why it helps

  • Clear approval routes for new AI use cases
  • Risk-tiered oversight that avoids both over-control and gaps

What you get

  • An AI use-case register and tiering methodology
  • Approval, escalation and monitoring procedures
  • Evidence templates ready for audit

05Assure

Problems surface while they are still cheap to fix.

Delivery AssuranceIndependent-minded assurance over automation and transformation delivery, so problems surface while they are still cheap to fix. 10Independent checkpoints

Overview

Delivery assurance provides a structured check on scope, quality, control design, testing and readiness across automation programmes.

Engagement

Gate-based reviews aligned to your delivery lifecycle, with a standing assurance cadence for longer programmes.

Why it helps

  • Early warning on scope creep and control gaps
  • Evidence of testing and sign-off for audit

What you get

  • Gate review reports with risk-rated findings
  • Tested control evidence
  • Documented readiness decisions
Process OptimisationReduce cycle time, rework and cost in operational processes, with control outcomes protected throughout. 09Evidence-led sprints

Overview

We apply lean and measurement-led methods to operational processes, using baseline data to identify waste, queueing and rework.

Engagement

Baseline measurement, targeted redesign of prioritised processes, and a measurement framework the client team keeps running after we leave.

Why it helps

  • Measured reduction in queue time and rework
  • Capacity released for higher-value investigation work

What you get

  • Baseline and target metrics for in-scope processes
  • Reduced rework and queue times
  • Sustained performance monitoring
